# Lessons

## 2026-07-22

- A zero-prior-mean discrepancy, a nonzero posterior conditional mean, and a parametric learned mean are three different claims. Analytically integrating zero-centered coefficients converts their marginal effect into covariance unless their conditional posterior contribution is recovered and shown.
- Febrianto et al.'s nonsensor formula must be read directly: the predictive mean is based on the conditioned FE state; the discrepancy kernel contributes new-location covariance in the published equation.
- Correlated spectral holdouts need contiguous blocks and guard bands. Random bins or adjacent-band training can make interpolation look like generalization.
- “Equal coverage” is not a complete preregistration rule unless calibration is nested or a numerical coverage tolerance is frozen before outer-test responses are seen.
