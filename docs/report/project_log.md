## 1. One-paragraph project summary
A harness-first proof of concept for **agentic scientific computing in structural
dynamics**: using AI agents (lead + helpers) to build a *robust Bayesian digital
twin* of aerospace structures that fuses a physics FEM with scrappy test data,
stays honest under model error (intervals widen and cover rather than giving a
confident wrong number), and pushes all the way to an engineering decision
(interface loads / vibration-test notch / force-limiting). Two tracks run in
parallel: (a) **method + science** (StatFEM, FEM modal twins, identifiability,
force-limited vibration per NASA-HDBK-7004C), and (b) **agentic harness research**
(can agents do trustworthy science; what gates / anti-overfitting protocols are
needed). Full vision: `notes/project_vision.md`.

## 2. Themes / workstreams
- **T1 — Synthetic 2DOF StatFEM/FBS** (method plumbing, sampler validation).
- **T2 — Real-data FEM modal twins** (ShockSat NASA FEM + test modes; MPL test stand).
- **T3 — Identifiability & experiment design** (FIM, profile likelihood, OSP, VOI).
- **T4 — Force-limited vibration / 7004C** (apparent mass, notch, multi-point 6-DOF).
- **T5 — Coupled primary/secondary QOI** (interface loads, method × coupling).
- **T6 — Agentic harness research** (blind vaults, anti-overfitting, critic-as-product,
  competitive multi-model runners, verification harness).
- **T7 — Teaching / outreach / IP** (teaching decks, blog drafts, patent dossiers, vision).

## 6. Cross-cutting findings (the report's "results")
1. **Identifiability is the binding constraint, repeatedly and consistently.** FIM
   (3 params), modal-twin NUTS (~4/9 groups), and the synthetic VOI (2/6 vs 5/6 mount
   combos) all show modal frequencies alone under-identify joints/mounts; the model-form
   discrepancy floor (σ_f ≈ 4–5%) — not sensor count — sets the limit.
2. **Held-out QOI prediction can stay well-calibrated even when parameters are
   multimodal/under-identified** — wide-but-covering bands are a feature of
   under-identification, and the held-out gate is the only detector.
3. **Single-point / net-force idealizations (7004C default) can be blind** to
   rocking and per-foot overtest; multi-point 6-DOF apparent mass is needed.
4. **The 7004C coupling shift is real and measurable on scrappy hardware** (ruler
   on the MPL shield, f1 −3.7% coupled vs solo).
5. **Agentic harness research:** anti-cheat gates shift failure to honest
   under-ambition, not fabrication; a hidden vault catches gate-passing overfit a
   convergence gate misses; a **separate critic pass** catches what authors and
   checkers miss ("the critic is the product"); the lead's problem-framing is now
   the weak link, not the worker.
