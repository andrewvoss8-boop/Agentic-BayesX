# Proposed closure-brief correction

> A Schneider-style additive frequency×location covariance was implemented for log-FRF magnitude. Its M2/M3 legacy TMCMC runs did not converge (M2 worst R-hat 2.577, minimum ESS 0.71; M3 worst R-hat 1.112, minimum ESS 16.95), and the discrepancy scale ran to about 12.4 in natural-log units. Schneider's rational FRF surrogate and a validated end-to-end reproduction were not implemented. The structured-mean study reported here separately tests correct zero-mean-GP conditioning and a learned conditional mean; its checker-backed terminal status and held-out effect sizes should be inserted before drawing the final closure claim.
