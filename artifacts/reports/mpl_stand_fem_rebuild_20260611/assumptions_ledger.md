## Uncertainty-type ledger (Assumptions & Uncertainty Gate)

- **Parameter uncertainty**: group scalars s_g (beam wall/rest, shield mem/bend, screw k, joint k, mount k, masses) — these are the future calibration targets; priors TBD in the sampler task.
- **Observation noise / model discrepancy / forcing**: NOT modeled here (no data, no sampler this task); the robust-twin demo plans HIGH assumed model discrepancy by design.
- **Boundary-condition uncertainty**: feet SPC vs grounding-spring variant (built, reported); shield screw stiffness (top knob).
- **Aleatory / build-to-build / test-to-test**: absent — single physical stand, zero datasets ingested.
- **QOI uncertainty**: QOI items (valve panel, electronics box, white box) carry ESTIMATED masses/locations/attachments → their local-mode frequencies are order-of-magnitude placements, not predictions.
