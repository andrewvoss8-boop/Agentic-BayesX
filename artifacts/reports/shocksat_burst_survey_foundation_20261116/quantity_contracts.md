# Quantity contracts

H (accelerance FRF): numerator response acceleration (g), denominator input force (lbf); unit g/lbf; free-free bungee configuration; drive point when response and force share node and direction. Limits: below the first elastic mode a drive point tends to the rigid-body mass line (about 0.008 to 0.02 g/lbf here); drive points alternate resonance and antiresonance. Magnitude only.

Median |dB| between two curves: median over frequency of |20 log10(|H_a|/|H_b|)|, dB. 6 dB is a factor of 2. Two variants: pixel grid (0.358 Hz) and power-averaged over 2.03 Hz (the line spacing of the project's shock caches) for like-for-like comparison with Card H.

corr: Pearson correlation of log10|H_a| and log10|H_b| over the band (1 = same shape, 0 = unrelated).

M1 (pre-registered): per curve, median |dB| between NASA test and NASA FEM, 46.79-258.38 Hz, 2.03 Hz averaged; summarised by the median over curves.

M2 (pre-registered, secondary): same against the frozen project FEM after whole-decade anchoring.

Lines across half-power width: 2 zeta f divided by the line spacing (dimensionless). Fewer than about 3 lines cannot support a damping estimate from an FRF.

Cross-orthogonality (NASA tables): mass-weighted overlap of a test and a FEM shape at the sensors, percent.
