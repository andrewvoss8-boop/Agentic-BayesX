## Literature framing used in the revision

- The ShockSat result is not that Bayesian updating is generally ineffective. The narrower claim is that
  **parameter updating inside the tested ShockSat model classes did not overcome the observed FRF shape error**.
  This is consistent with the classical warning that updating cannot repair physics excluded from the chosen
  parameterization (R2-R3).
- The delivered discrepancy-aware band follows the literature's reason for modeling the model-reality gap
  (R5-R6), while the unvalidated normal direction is kept explicitly caveated.
- The evidence-selected remodel result does not invalidate Bayesian evidence. Evidence answers which candidate
  best explains the observed data under the specified model set, prior, and likelihood (R7). Held-out predictive
  skill is a separate target (R8), and ShockSat exposed a gap between them.
- The report's coverage, blinding/humility, held-out-drive, and baseline checks are presented as a project-specific
  credibility scorecard aligned with the general V&V/UQ objective (R9), not as an established standard.
- The advisor progress report defines NUTS and its diagnostics from primary sources (R12-R13) and names TMCMC
  only as an established alternative for difficult targets (R16). It explicitly states that no completed
  ShockSat TMCMC posterior earned the report's convergence and held-out requirements.
- The 2022 bridge digital-twin paper (R14), rather than an incorrectly dated 2021 citation, is used as a positive
  statFEM comparator. Its dense strain-sensing/static-inertia assumptions are not equated to ShockSat's resonant,
  indirect-force setting.
