## TL;DR verdict (checker-generated from the numbers)
**ROBUSTNESS-WIN, with a clear partial sharpness WIN on the peak QOI.** Putting the physics in the PRIOR with a
learned trust temperature β (Information Field Theory) gives a twin that, on the SAME real data / forward model /
held-out test / proper scores as our StatFEM arms:
- **CONVERGES reproducibly** (under TMCMC) and stays **bounded** where the StatFEM whole-FRF arm M2 (physics in
  the LIKELIHOOD, free σ_d) **diverges** with a variance singularity — the robustness claim, on the identical
  field observable;
- **beats the working L5 twin on the peak-|H| QOI** on all three proper scores AND coverage;
- **loses to L5 on band-GRMS** (β collapses → physics-derived band variance over-widens);
- reads a **β-thermometer of ~246× distrust** of the coarse FEM — quantifying, from the trust side, the
  model-form error the StatFEM run found qualitatively.
This is NOT a clean sharpness win, and it cannot be: the coarse FEM is the binding ceiling (pre-committed). The
honest result is that physics-in-prior is **more robust and better-calibrated on the peak QOI**, not uniformly
sharper.

## 1. Method summary (the IFT twin)
- **Observable (SAME as StatFEM M2):** log|H| accelerance magnitude [g/lbf], real single-impact FRF of hit-001,
  band 45–260 Hz (~172 lines), DR-eligible mapped channels (peak/floor ≥ 30×), bad channels ch101/ch29–31
  excluded.
- **Forward (reused byte-identical):** reduced dynamic-stiffness FRF, 74-dim, M=I, K(θ)=A_fix+Σ_gθ_gA_g,
  hysteretic η=2ζ, ζ=0.01.
- **IFT construction:** physics-informed PRIOR on the complex field u, π(u|θ,β) ∝ exp(−(β/2)‖A(θ,ω)u−f‖²),
  A=(1+iη)K(θ)−ω²I, f=unit force at the drive node. β = learned inverse-temperature = trust in the physics.
- **Likelihood (the documented moment approximation, Andrew's directive D0):** log|H_j| is log-Rician (the field
  is Gaussian but |·| is nonlinear), so we marginalize the field by matching its first two moments —
  **log|H_j^meas|(ω) ~ N( log|H_j^FEM(θ)| + c , σ_meas² + v_j(ω;θ,β)/(2|H_j^FEM(θ)|²) )** — a log|H| likelihood
  whose per-bin variance is PHYSICS-DERIVED and set by ONE learned trust scalar β. σ_meas=0.15; c=global gain.
  Exact at high SNR; weak only in deep antiresonances (already downweighted). Full derivation: `method_ift.md`.
- **Inferred:** θ on the 4 FIM-identifiable groups (bottom_box, top_box, plates, cross_beam), β, global gain c.
  **Fixed:** ζ=0.01, σ_meas=0.15, the other 5 θ groups =1, correlation/operator form.
- **Data count:** 1 structure, 1 impact location, 1 hit (hit-001), ~80 DR-eligible channels split obs/held-out
  per seed, ≥5 IFT / 25 L5 / 3 M2 seeds. No repeated tests/flights (build/test variability NOT modeled — YELLOW).

## 3. Apples-to-apples (the whole point)
IFT, L5 and M2 differ ONLY in WHERE the physics/trust lives. Everything else is byte-identical and REUSED, proven
by `manifest_sha256.txt` (15 files; `check.py` re-verifies): same data, same forward (`realfrf`/`core`), same
split RNG (`heldout_qoi` logic, obs_frac 0.6, DR≥30), same scorer (`score.py`), same convergence gate.
- **IFT** = physics in the PRIOR (β), whole-FRF log|H|.
- **M2** = physics in the LIKELIHOOD (Schneider σ_d × freq×space discrepancy), whole-FRF log|H| (the direct
  apples-to-apples contrast). [M2 computed Kronecker-efficiently = dense `real_arms.M2` to 2e-15; `m2_arm.py`.]
- **L5** = StatFEM modal-feature (peak frequencies — a DIFFERENT, unimodal observable) + marginal QOI
  discrepancy. The practical sharpness BAR (the only whole-data-converged twin).

## 5. Convergence + the robustness story (`fig_convergence_table`)
Both whole-FRF arms are **multimodal under NUTS** (the likelihood mean is the oscillatory FEM log|H(θ)| that many
θ fit): IFT-NUTS R̂ 264.9 (0 div), M2-NUTS R̂ 104–115 (StatFEM). Under **TMCMC** (the multimodal-capable
escalation, copied `tmcmc.py`, pre-authorized by the card §2 / StatFEM D9):
- **IFT-TMCMC reproduces** the multimodal posterior: cross-run R̂ **1.000–1.001**, ESS 1143–1613, over 4
  independent runs, all 5 splits — CONVERGED in the documented TMCMC sense, β included.
- **M2-TMCMC does NOT** (StatFEM D10: cross-run R̂ 129, σ_d→7.4 "variance→∞" singularity, interval ~1e17).
The difference is structural: IFT's β is bounded by the physics + its prior; M2's σ_d is free and runs away. =
physics-in-prior is **more robust** than physics-in-likelihood on the identical field observable.

## 6. β as a model-form-error thermometer (`figB_beta`) + identifiability
β collapses to a posterior median ~0.174 = **~246× below** the forward-model nominal β* (≈42.8), consistently
across all 5 splits (0.162–0.181), and stays **bounded** (no runaway). Reading: the twin learns **strong distrust
of the coarse FEM** and widens gracefully — the trust-side quantification of the model-form ceiling. β is
**well-identified** here (weakly-identified fraction 0/5), unlike the consistency toy's weak-β-above-threshold
finding — on real data the data pins β at the collapsed value.
